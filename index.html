01 // About

I work where operational reality meets practical data engineering. Instead of focusing only on pre-cleaned sandbox datasets, my background comes from fast-paced, high-volume production facilities—where data usually starts fragmented across desktop spreadsheets, handwritten tallies, or rigid ERP ledgers.

Over the past few years, I have built central data pipelines from scratch, scaled database architectures to prevent local file crashes, and supported enterprise ERP deployments. I specialize in identifying where numbers deviate from physical reality, automating manual reporting routines, and turning operational logs into interactive BI dashboards that decision-makers actually rely on.

Today, my focus centers on building reliable data pipelines, operational modeling, and actionable business intelligence. I am actively open to full-time opportunities and strategic projects where practical field knowledge and clean analytics drive measurable business impact.

I standardized data entry structures, authored automated ingestion triggers using Google Apps Script to pipe verified daily rows directly into Google BigQuery, and built auto-updating Looker Studio dashboards for live executive monitoring.

As daily transaction history scaled into tens of thousands of rows, master Excel workbooks regularly froze and crashed local office machines.
